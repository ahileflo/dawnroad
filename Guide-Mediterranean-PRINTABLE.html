The Big Idea

Why this loop wins

Most people drive Europe in straight lines just to tick countries. This guide does the opposite: one intelligent loop where the drive down shows you one half of the continent and the drive back a completely different half — while your daily spend drops the further south-east you go.

The cost gradient is the whole trick. The expensive north (Italy, Slovenia) is transit and scenery where you cook for yourself. The cheap south-east (Montenegro, Albania, North Macedonia) is where you stop the engine, eat out twice a day for the price of one Western lunch, and sleep by the sea for free.

Headline budget: ~€30–40/day for two once you're in the south-east; transit days higher. A 3–4 week trip realistically lands around €1,200–1,800 for two including fuel. Verify current fuel/toll prices.
The Route

Your loop, country by country

A clockwise-ish loop — adapt it to your start point and time. Down the Adriatic, deep into the Balkans, back through the mountains.

🇮🇹 Italy — the Adriatic transit pricey

Efficient, scenic transit south. Don't blow the budget here — this is "cook in the van" territory.

Worth a stop: Lake Garda & the Dolomites (detour north), Venice (park on the mainland, take the tram/boat in), the Adriatic beaches, and — if you have time to go far south — Puglia's white trulli of Alberobello and the cliffs of Polignano a Mare.

ZTL warning: Italian historic centres have camera-enforced "Limited Traffic Zones." Drive into one and you get a fine by post weeks later. Park outside and walk in. Always.

Ferries to the south-east leave from Ancona, Bari and Brindisi (to Greece/Albania) — see the Ferry Strategy section.

🇸🇮 Slovenia — tiny & stunning mid

A jewel-box detour. Lake Bled and quieter Lake Bohinj, the turquoise Soča Valley, Triglav National Park, leafy Ljubljana, and the Venetian coastal town of Piran.

Vignette required for motorways — buy the e-vignette online or at a border station before you drive, or risk a heavy fine.

🇭🇷 Croatia — the famous coast mid

Plitvice Lakes and Krka waterfalls, the Dalmatian cities of Zadar, Split (Diocletian's Palace) and Dubrovnik, plus countless islands.

Wild camping is illegal and enforced in Croatia — use official campsites here. Croatia uses the Euro (since 2023) and has motorway tolls.

🇲🇪 Montenegro — where it gets cheap cheap

The drama begins. Bay of Kotor (Kotor old town + tiny Perast), the soaring Lovćen serpentine road, Durmitor National Park and the vast Tara Canyon, and birdlife-rich Lake Skadar.

Money/border: Montenegro uses the Euro (unofficially) but is outside the EU — you'll need your passport and a vehicle green card insurance extension.

🇦🇱 Albania — the heart of the trip ⭐ very cheap

The budget jewel: stunning, hospitable, and incredibly affordable. Spend most of your time here.

  • The Accursed Mountains: Shkodër → Theth & Valbona (the famous valley-to-valley hike, plus the Blue Eye of Theth).
  • UNESCO old towns: Berat ("city of a thousand windows") and stone-built Gjirokastër.
  • The Albanian Riviera: Dhërmi, Himarë, Borsh, the islands of Ksamil, the remote Gjipe cove, and the Blue Eye spring near Sarandë.
  • Butrint: ancient ruins near the Corfu ferry.
Money: currency is the Lek (carry cash). Meals €5–10. Free/tolerated camping is relaxed — still arrive late, leave early, leave no trace.

🇲🇰 North Macedonia — the way back cheap

Loop home inland and your euro doubles again. Lake Ohrid (UNESCO, one of Europe's oldest lakes), Matka Canyon, and lively Skopje. Currency: the Denar.

🇬🇷 Greece (Ionian edge) — optional mid

If you dip in: the clifftop monasteries of Meteora, lakeside Ioannina, and the Ionian coast/islands.

Greece tightened wild-camping enforcement — fines are real. Use campsites or clearly tolerated spots and check the latest local rules.

Logistics

Tolls, vignettes & ferries

Know the rules before each border

  • Vignette countries (buy a sticker/e-vignette first): e.g. Slovenia, Austria, Hungary, Switzerland, Czechia.
  • Pay-per-distance tolls: e.g. Italy, Croatia, Greece.
  • Non-EU borders (Montenegro, Albania, N. Macedonia, Bosnia): passport + vehicle green card insurance.

⛴️ Ferry strategy — can save ~2,000 km

Instead of driving the whole coast twice, take a ferry for one long leg (e.g. an Adriatic crossing from Italy, or the short Sarandë–Corfu hop). Price the ferry against fuel + tolls + days of driving — it often wins. Book ahead in summer.

Sleep

How to find safe, free camping

This method beats any fixed list, because spots and rules change constantly:

  1. Park4Night — crowd-sourced spots with recent reviews, photos, services. Filter free / van / quiet, and read the latest reviews for safety + legality.
  2. iOverlander — great for wilder, off-grid spots.
  3. Golden rules: arrive late, leave early, no chairs/awnings out where it's merely "tolerated," one night per spot, leave no trace, trust recent reviews + your gut.
  4. Services (fresh water / grey + toilet dump): plan one every 2–3 days.
When unsure, €5–10 to park behind a taverna buys a meal, goodwill, and a safe night.
Self-Sufficiency

The off-grid survival kit

⚡ Power

  • LiFePO4 leisure battery (100Ah+), solar panel (120W+), and a DC-DC charger as your cloudy-day backup (charges as you drive).
  • Scenario — dead battery, no sun: drive an hour (DC-DC tops you up) or pay for a campsite with shore power.

💧 Water

  • Fresh + grey tanks, collapsible jerry cans, and an inline carbon filter. Refill at fuel stations, cemeteries, springs, campsites.

🍳 Food & cooking

  • Gas hob or induction + fridge. Shop local markets (cheaper + better). Keep a 3-day non-perishable buffer.

🚽 Toilet & waste

  • Cassette or composting toilet; know your dump points; never empty illegally — it's the #1 way vans get banned from areas.

🛡️ Safety scenarios

  • Theft: never leave valuables visible; park in well-reviewed spots; a steering lock deters.
  • Breakdown: carry pan-European breakdown cover; know your green-card validity in non-EU countries.
  • Medical: EU GHIC/EHIC for EU stops + travel insurance that covers non-EU Balkans; a stocked first-aid kit; pharmacies are excellent and cheap in the Balkans.
  • Weather/heat: insulation, blackout, a 12V fan, park in shade; in mountains, nights get cold — carry warm layers even in summer.
  • Connectivity: a travel eSIM or local SIM + offline maps downloaded before you lose signal.
Before You Go

Pre-departure checklist

  • Vignettes & tolls mapped for every country (2026 prices)
  • Ferry leg priced + booked (if using one)
  • Park4Night + iOverlander installed; offline maps downloaded
  • Van serviced — tyres, brakes, fluids; confirmed under 3.5t
  • Insurance + breakdown cover valid in all countries; green card for non-EU
  • Passports valid; cash in Lek (Albania) & Denar (N. Macedonia)
  • Power/water tested off-grid for 3 days at home first
  • First-aid kit, gas/CO alarm, fire extinguisher checked
