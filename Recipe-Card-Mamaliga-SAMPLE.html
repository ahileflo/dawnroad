Mămăligă

🇷🇴 Romanian polenta · 🍽️ serves 2 · ⏱️ 15 min · ⭐ very easy
📷 add your photo: a bowl of mămăligă

You need

💧 3 cups water 🧂 1 tsp salt 🌽 1 cup cornmeal 🧀 cheese (optional)

Do this

1
💧🔥

Put water + salt in the pot. Heat until it bubbles.

2
🌽🥄

Slowly pour in the cornmeal. Stir, stir, stir.

3
⏳🥄

Keep stirring ~10 min until thick. Add cheese on top.

🧤 Hot pot! Ask a grown-up to help with the stove.
🎉🍽️

Done! Enjoy your mămăligă.
