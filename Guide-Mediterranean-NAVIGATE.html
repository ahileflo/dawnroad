The Strategy

Drive the loop, spend less the further you go

Expensive north = transit (cook in the van). Cheap south-east = holiday (eat out, sleep free). Target ~€30–40/day for two once you're past Montenegro.

Carry These

Off-grid & safety essentials

Power: LiFePO4 battery + solar + DC-DC charger. Water: tanks + inline filter. Safety: CO/gas alarm, first aid, travel insurance valid in non-EU Balkans, green card. Connectivity: travel eSIM + offline maps downloaded before you lose signal.

Travel smart

Safety, kids, pets & groups

🛡️ Stay safe

Park in well-reviewed spots, keep valuables out of sight, and a steering lock deters trouble. Carry pan-European breakdown cover, a green card for non-EU borders, and travel insurance valid in the non-EU Balkans (plus GHIC/EHIC for EU stops). Pack a first-aid kit and a CO/gas alarm.

👶 Travelling with kids

Fit proper child seats, plan shorter driving days with frequent breaks, and favour spots with shade, space and facilities. Book a campsite with a playground every few nights to reset — and keep snacks, water and entertainment within reach.

🐾 Travelling with pets

You'll need a microchip + valid rabies vaccination and an EU pet passport (or EU Animal Health Certificate); some countries also require tapeworm treatment. Non-EU borders (Albania, Montenegro, North Macedonia) have their own entry rules. Never leave a pet in a hot van, and note vet locations along the route.

⚠️ Pet & border rules change — confirm current requirements for every country before you travel.

👥 Groups & organised trips (school, clubs)

In a convoy? Share a live location, agree clear meeting points, nominate one "lead van," and book group-friendly campsites ahead. Split costs fairly with the free Dawnroad budget planner.

🏳️‍🌈 LGBTQ+ travellers

Big cities and most of the coast are relaxed and welcoming; some rural/conservative pockets across the Balkans are less so. The whole route is very doable — just read the local vibe, and we flag the welcoming hubs. Everyone's invited on this road. 💛
