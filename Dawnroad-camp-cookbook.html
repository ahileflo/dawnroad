2. Mămăligă (Romanian polenta)

🇷🇴 serves 2 · ⏱️ 15 min · ⭐ easy
📷 add a photo
💧 3 cups water🧂 salt🌽 1 cup cornmeal🧀 cheese
1
💧🔥

Boil water + salt in a pot.

2
🌽🥄

Slowly pour in cornmeal. Stir, stir, stir.

3
⏳🧀

Stir ~10 min until thick. Add cheese. Done!

🧤 Hot pot! Ask a grown-up to help with the stove.

🍳 serves 2 · ⏱️ 15 min · ⭐ easy
📷 add a photo
🥫 tin tomatoes🧅 onion🥚 2–3 eggs🫒 oil · 🧂 salt
1
🧅🍳

Cook chopped onion in oil until soft.

2
🥫🔥

Add tomatoes + salt. Let it bubble 5 min.

3
🥚⏳

Crack eggs on top. Lid on, ~5 min until set. Eat with bread.
